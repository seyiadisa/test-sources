Welcome to Open School University

Founded in 1985, Open School University has established itself as a premier institution of higher learning, dedicated to academic excellence, innovative research, and the holistic development of students. Our sprawling 250-acre campus provides a vibrant learning environment where tradition meets innovation.

At Open School University, we believe in nurturing not just academic prowess but also critical thinking, creativity, and leadership qualities. Our world-class faculty comprises distinguished scholars, researchers, and industry experts who are committed to providing quality education that prepares students for the challenges of tomorrow.

With state-of-the-art facilities, cutting-edge laboratories, extensive library resources, and a diverse student community from over 60 countries, Open School University offers an unparalleled educational experience. Our commitment to affordable quality education ensures that financial constraints do not hinder deserving students from achieving their dreams.

Below, you will find detailed information about tuition fees for the 2025/2026 academic year across our seven distinguished faculties. We offer flexible payment plans and numerous scholarship opportunities to support our students' academic journey.

Faculty of Engineering

The Faculty of Engineering at Open School University is renowned for producing innovative engineers who drive technological advancement globally. Our comprehensive programs cover Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, and Chemical Engineering. With industry partnerships, modern laboratories equipped with the latest technology, and a curriculum that balances theoretical knowledge with practical application, our graduates are highly sought after by leading corporations worldwide. Students benefit from internship opportunities, research projects, and exposure to real-world engineering challenges.

Annual Tuition Fee

$18,500
• Includes laboratory fees and equipment usage
• Access to engineering workshops and maker spaces
• Industry mentorship programs included
• Payment plans available over 3 semesters

Faculty of Medicine

Our Faculty of Medicine is accredited by international medical bodies and offers comprehensive training in various medical disciplines. The program combines rigorous academic instruction with extensive clinical experience at our affiliated teaching hospitals. Students receive hands-on training under the supervision of experienced physicians and surgeons. Our curriculum emphasizes patient-centered care, medical ethics, research methodology, and community health. The faculty boasts advanced simulation labs, a well-stocked medical library, and partnerships with leading healthcare institutions. Graduates from our medical program have an excellent track record in licensing examinations and residency placements.

Annual Tuition Fee

$27,000
• Clinical rotations at partner hospitals included
• Access to medical simulation centers
• Comprehensive health insurance provided
• Medical equipment and supplies included
• International medical conference attendance opportunities

Faculty of Law

The Faculty of Law at Open School University offers a rigorous legal education that prepares students for successful careers in various legal fields. Our curriculum covers constitutional law, criminal law, corporate law, international law, and emerging areas such as cyber law and environmental law. Students participate in moot court competitions, legal clinics, and internships with prestigious law firms and judicial bodies. Our distinguished faculty includes practicing attorneys, judges, and legal scholars who bring real-world experience to the classroom. The faculty's law library houses an extensive collection of legal texts, journals, and digital resources. Graduates are well-prepared for bar examinations and legal practice.

Annual Tuition Fee

$16,800
• Access to comprehensive legal databases
• Moot court and trial advocacy training
• Legal clinic experience included
• Bar exam preparation support
• Networking events with legal professionals

Faculty of Experimental Sciences

The Faculty of Experimental Sciences emphasizes hands-on learning through laboratory work, field studies, and experimental research. Programs include Biology, Chemistry, Geology, Oceanography, and Materials Science. Students develop strong practical skills in experimental design, data collection, analysis, and interpretation. Our extensive laboratory facilities include electron microscopes, spectroscopy equipment, climate-controlled growth chambers, and field research stations. The faculty encourages interdisciplinary collaboration and innovation. Students regularly participate in field trips, research expeditions, and collaborative projects with other institutions. Graduates are well-prepared for careers in research laboratories, quality control, environmental monitoring, and scientific consulting.

Annual Tuition Fee

$13,800
• Field trip expenses covered
• Laboratory safety equipment provided
• Research materials and reagents included
• Access to field research stations
• Scientific publication support
